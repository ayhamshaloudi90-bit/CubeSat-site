Mission concept

Overview

The problem

A 6U CubeSat at 600 km SSO takes 25+ years to naturally deorbit — creating a collision hazard and violating ESA's new 5-year Zero Debris rule.

The solution

An integrated drag sail deploys end-of-mission, increasing the cross-section by 10×. An AI module picks the optimal window based on solar flux forecasts.
