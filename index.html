Overview

The problem

A 6U CubeSat at 600 km SSO takes 25+ years to naturally deorbit — creating a collision hazard and violating ESA's new 5-year Zero Debris rule.

The solution

An integrated drag sail deploys end-of-mission. An on-board AI selects the optimal window based on solar flux forecasts, capitalising on atmospheric drag.

Physics Deep-Dive: The Drag Equation

The deorbit force is calculated as $F_D = \frac{1}{2} \rho v^2 C_D A$. At 600km, the gas density ($\rho$) is extremely low, but our sail increases the cross-sectional area ($A$) by 1000%, effectively turning the "vacuum" of space into a thin fluid that slows the craft down.
