Mission concept

Overview

The problem

A 6U CubeSat at 600 km SSO takes 25+ years to naturally deorbit — violating ESA's new 5-year Zero Debris rule. Propulsion adds too much cost and complexity for most CubeSats.

The solution

An integrated drag sail deploys end-of-mission. An on-board AI module selects the optimal deployment window based on solar flux forecasts, cutting deorbit time to under 5 years.
